# Privacy policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

Mushak does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The application
has no telemetry, analytics, advertising, remote service, or update checker.

Mushak communicates locally with a supported Logitech mouse through Windows USB
or Bluetooth APIs. It stores settings in `%APPDATA%\Mushak\config.toml` and may
write diagnostic logs under `%APPDATA%\Mushak\logs`. These files remain on the
computer unless the user chooses to share them for support.

Uninstalling Mushak does not remove these user files. They can be deleted at any
time after Mushak has exited.

Questions about this policy can be filed at
<https://github.com/divyangchauhan/Mushak/issues>.
