# Mouse artwork

The diagrams share artwork where the button layout is the same:

- Original MX Master and MX Master 2S use `mx-master-2s.png`.
- MX Master 3 and MX Master 3S use `mx-master-3s.png`.
- MX Master 4 uses `mx-master-4.png`.

Mac and Business variants use their generation's diagram. Artwork represents
button positions, not every finish or surface detail.

`mx-master-2s.png` is Logitech's graphite MX Master 2S product image, downloaded
unchanged from https://secure.logitech.com/assets/65106/3/mx-master-2s.png on
2026-10-22. Logitech's support gallery identifies this product at
https://support.logi.com/hc/en-150/articles/360026446033-GalleryImage-MX-Master-2S-Primary-Image.

`mx-master-3s.png` is the graphite 3S front and thumb-side product render from
[Logitech's product page](https://www.logitech.com/en-us/shop/p/mx-master-3s),
downloaded on 2026-10-24 at 1000 pixels wide using Logitech's image service:
https://resource.logitech.com/w_1000,f_png/content/dam/logitech/en/products/mice/mx-master-3s/2025-update/mx-master-3s-bluetooth-edition-left-front-view-graphite-new-8.png.

`mx-master-4.png` is the graphite 4 front and thumb-side product render from
[Logitech's product page](https://www.logitech.com/en-us/shop/p/mx-master-4.910-007560),
downloaded on 2026-10-24 at 1000 pixels wide using Logitech's image service:
https://resource.logitech.com/w_1000,f_png/content/dam/logitech/en/products/mice/mx-master-4/gallery/mx-master-4-graphite-front-3qtr-angle-gallery-10.png.
